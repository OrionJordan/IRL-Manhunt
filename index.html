HOW TO PLAY

  1. Set your Name: Enter your callsign in the top box and tap SAVE.
  2. Room Lobby: Anyone can tap CREATE ROOM or enter a room code. The room runs completely hostless over an instant decentralized mesh!
  3. Host Badge: The player who creates the room is badged with 👑 HOST so everyone knows who the match leader is.

  5. Targeting: The compass points directly to the physically closest opponent on the other team.
