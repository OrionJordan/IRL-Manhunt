HOW TO PLAY

  1. Set your Name: Enter your callsign in the top box and tap SAVE.
  2. Form a Lobby: Player 1 taps Create Lobby. Up to 15 other players join using the QR code or 6-digit room code.
  3. Choose Teams: Tap SEEKER or HIDER. You can have any team distribution (e.g., 3 Seekers vs. 1 Hider).
  4. Lock Onto Opponents: Your compass automatically points directly to the physically closest opponent on the other team.
  5. 15-Foot Nether Spin: When you get within 15 feet of your target, the needle enters erratic Nether spin! Seekers see SEEK and Hiders see HIDE!
